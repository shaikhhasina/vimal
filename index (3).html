Eight cleaners for every corner of the house

Tap "How to use" on any product for quick directions.

Advanced Toilet Cleaner

Powerful cleaning for a germ-free toilet. 350ml, 9.9X strength.

  • Removes limescale, rust & tough stains
  • Deep coverage under the rim
  • Fresh, clean fragrance

Apply along the bowl rim, leave for 10–15 minutes, brush lightly and flush.

All-Surface Bathroom Cleaner

Removes soap scum, hard water spots and grime.

  • Fast-acting on tiles and basins
  • Safe for ceramic and chrome
  • Slows dirt build-up

Spray on the surface, leave for 2 minutes, scrub gently and rinse.

Ultra Dish Wash Gel

Tough on grease, gentle on hands. Lemon fragrance.

  • Concentrated formula
  • High foam, shiny utensils
  • pH balanced

Apply directly on a wet sponge, or dilute 1 teaspoon in water.

Refreshing Floor Cleaner

Deep clean with no sticky residue.

  • Cleans & shines
  • Suits all floor types
  • Long-lasting fragrance

Mix 15 ml (one capful) in 4 litres of water for regular mopping.

Creamy Handwash

Soft on skin, effective every day.

  • Rich moisturising lather
  • Gentle on hands
  • Removes grease & impurities

Pump a small amount on wet hands, rub for 20 seconds and rinse.

Premium Disinfectant Phenyl

Deep clean, odour control and floor care.

  • Disinfects surfaces
  • Neutralises foul odours
  • For floors & drains

Add a few drops in water for mopping, or use undiluted in outdoor drains.

Power-Wash Washing Powder

Bright clothes, powerful cleaning.

  • Lifts deep dirt
  • Keeps whites bright
  • Protects coloured clothes

Use 1 scoop for regular loads and 1.5 scoops for heavily soiled clothes.

Streak-Free Glass Cleaner

Crystal clear, spotless shine.

  • Quick-drying
  • Removes fingerprints and dust
  • No cloudy streaks
